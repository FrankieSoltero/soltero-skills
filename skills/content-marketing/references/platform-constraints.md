## LinkedIn mechanics (from agency-agents; algorithm claims [UNSOURCED])

- Paragraphs 2–3 lines max; break at a tension point before the "see more" fold.
- 3–5 specific hashtags max.
- External links in comments, not post body [UNSOURCED — widely believed].
- Carousels: native PDF upload; ≤15 words/slide; slide 1 works standalone.
- Reply to comments in the first hour [UNSOURCED].
- Post archetypes with skeletons: story (moment→tension→resolution→
  transferable insight) · expertise (common mistake→correct mental model→
  proof) · opinion (take→acknowledge counter→defend→invite reply) · data
  (surprising number→why→one implication).
- Draft 3 hook variants per post: curiosity gap / bold claim / specific story.

## X/Twitter mechanics

- First line is the whole battle; no hashtag soup (0–2 max).
- Threads: one idea per tweet; number only if >4 tweets; end with the CTA.

## General

- One artifact = one CTA.
- Every post needs a defensible point of view; neutral content gets neutral
  results (agency-agents craft note — judgment, not data).
